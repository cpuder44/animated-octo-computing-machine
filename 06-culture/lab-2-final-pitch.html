01 / Strategy

The product promise sets the evaluation standard.

Career-fit intelligence that recognizes transferable experience without treating unverified qualifications as established facts.

Trust metric 01

Transferable Skill Recognition

Find relevant expertise even when a candidate's prior title or industry differs from the target role.

Trust metric 02

Evidence-Grounded Fit Explanations

Every material fit claim should trace to candidate evidence and job requirements.

Trust metric 03

High-Fit Recommendation Precision

Reserve the strongest recommendations for opportunities that actually meet fit criteria.

Strategic trade-off: Keep unconventional paths visible as EXPLORATORY when evidence is incomplete; do not inflate the STRONG MATCH label.

Rubric change: grounding over fluency

  • Verify material claims individually against sources.
  • Do not penalize length when a response is grounded.
  • Fail material eligibility or business claims with no source support.
  • Do not reward persuasive wording for unsupported conclusions.

Capstone / Reflection

“Good enough” depends on the risk.

A high pass rate does not necessarily mean a product is ready to scale. We need to identify which failures matter most and build controls that prevent those failures without limiting RoleSignal's ability to discover unconventional career opportunities.
